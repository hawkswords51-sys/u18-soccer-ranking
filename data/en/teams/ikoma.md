## intro

Ikoma High School (生駒高校) is a prefectural state school in Ikoma, a city in the north-west of Nara Prefecture on the border with Osaka. It opened in 1963 and teaches the general academic course only. Its football team reached a national tournament for the first time in 2022, when it won the Nara qualifiers for the summer Inter-High, and it returned to the Inter-High in 2024. On 7 November 2026 it won the Nara qualifying tournament for the winter All Japan High School Soccer Tournament for the first time, beating Unebi 1-0 in the final, and will play at the winter championship for the first time in 2026–27. In the semi-finals it beat Nara Ikuei, who had won the previous five Nara titles, so a team other than Nara Ikuei will represent the prefecture for the first time in six editions.

## style

The head coach, who arrived at Ikoma in 2017, is a former professional goalkeeper. He played his youth football in Nara, went to the national winter championship as a student at Miminashi High School, studied at Osaka University of Health and Sport Sciences, and then played for Tokushima Vortis from 2005 to 2008, making 26 appearances in J2. After retiring he became a teacher and coached football at several schools in Nara.

The 2026 qualifying tournament was won with tight defending. Ikoma, one of the four seeds, entered in the third round and beat Koriyama 12-1. It then won its last three matches 1-0. In the quarter-finals it beat Sakurai. In the semi-final against Nara Ikuei on 1 November, a second-year midfielder crossed from the left in the 13th minute and a third-year midfielder headed in. In the second half Nara Ikuei were given a penalty, and the captain, a third-year goalkeeper, saved it. He suffered cramp in the process and was carried off, and the reserve goalkeeper kept the clean sheet. The coach said afterwards that the theme of the day had been "smile": "it was not about winning or losing". In the final against Unebi, the Inter-High qualifying champions, the score was 0-0 at half-time. In the 57th minute the same second-year midfielder scored from a pass by the same third-year, and Ikoma held on. Unebi had beaten Ikoma 1-0 in the Inter-High semi-finals in May and 4-0 in both of their prefectural league matches, and the coach had called his team "the challengers" before the final.

## history

- **1963** — The school opens as a prefectural general high school.
- **2017** — Ikoma reaches the final of the Nara Inter-High qualifiers and loses to Ichijo on penalties after a 2-2 draw, then plays at the Kinki regional tournament. The current head coach arrives the same year.
- **2017–2021** — Regular semi-finals and quarter-finals in both Nara qualifying tournaments, without a title.
- **2022** — Ikoma wins the Nara Inter-High qualifiers, beating Nara Ikuei 2-1 in the semi-finals and Kashiba 4-0 in the final, and reaches a national tournament for the first time in its 39th year. It loses 0-3 to Shohei in the first round in Tokushima. The coach said the gap was "individual quality and physical strength". The team also finishes first in Nara's Division 1 and loses 1-5 to Omi's second team in the Prince League Kansai play-offs in December.
- **2023** — Ikoma reaches its first winter qualifying final and loses 1-2 to Nara Ikuei after extra time. In February 2024 it loses the Nara schools' new-season tournament final to Nara Ikuei 1-3, again after extra time.
- **2024** — Ikoma beats Nara Ikuei 2-0 in the Inter-High qualifying final on 8 June, scoring twice from two shots, and returns to the Inter-High. At J-Village in Fukushima it enters in the second round and loses 0-3 to Aomori Yamada. In November it loses the winter qualifying final to Nara Ikuei 1-3.
- **2025** — Semi-final defeat by Nara Ikuei (2-4) in the Inter-High qualifiers and a third-round defeat by Ichijo (2-3) in the winter qualifiers.
- **2026** — One-goal semi-final defeats in both the new-season tournament (2-3 to Nara Ikuei) and the Inter-High qualifiers (0-1 to Unebi), then the first winter qualifying title, with 1-0 wins over Nara Ikuei and Unebi.

## model

For readers new to Japanese youth football: high-school teams and J.League academies play in the same national league pyramid. At the top are the U-18 Premier League (EAST and WEST), then nine regional Prince Leagues, then the prefectural leagues. Ikoma plays in Division 1 of Nara's prefectural U-18 league (the "NFA League"), one level below the Prince League Kansai. Two high-school tournaments carry even more prestige than the league: the summer Inter-High and the winter championship, which is televised nationally and ends at the National Stadium in January.

- **A state school.** Ikoma is a prefectural school with a general academic course, not a private football school. Its national appearances have come in the same years as rivalries with Nara Ikuei, a private school that has dominated Nara's winter qualifiers, and with Unebi, another prefectural school.
- **Two teams in the league.** The first team plays in Division 1 and a second team, "Ikoma 2nd", plays in Division 2, which gives more players regular competitive matches.
- **A goalkeeper as coach.** The head coach is a former J2 goalkeeper, and the goalkeepers decided the 2026 semi-final: the captain saved a penalty and his replacement finished the match without conceding.

## alumni

No Ikoma graduate is known to have played professionally. One former player went on to Komazawa University and in 2011 played for Nara Club, which was then in the Kansai regional league, before it joined the J.League. Kyogo Furuhashi grew up in the city of Ikoma, but he went to Kokoku High School in Osaka and is not an Ikoma graduate. No Ikoma graduate was in Japan's squad for the 2026 World Cup.
