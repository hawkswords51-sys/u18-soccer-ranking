## intro

Kawasaki Frontale U-18 is the under-18 team of Kawasaki Frontale, and the academy that produced TANAKA Ao, ITAKURA Kou, MITOMA Kaoru and TAKAI Kota. It was set up in 1998, the year after the club took the Frontale name, in a region where Yomiuri and Nissan had been developing players for years already. For a long time it was the newcomer. In 2022, in its first season in the Premier League, it won the EAST division. The team trains at Anker Frontown Ikuta in Tama Ward, Kawasaki, a site shared by every age group from under-12 to under-18.

## style

Kawasaki's football is built on two verbs the club uses constantly: tomeru and keru — to stop the ball and to kick it. The idea, which became the language of the whole club under the first-team coach of the early 2010s, is that a player who can stop the ball exactly where he wants it under pressure creates time and space for himself, and can then pass accurately into it. Every age group is coached in the same terms, so that a player promoted to the first team already knows the vocabulary. MORI Yusuke, head coach since 2025, has kept that technical base and added intensity: winning second balls and the duels at the edge of the play.

## history

- **1998** — The under-18 and under-15 teams are founded, one year after the club became Kawasaki Frontale. The goal from the start is players who can compete technically, rather than a team that wins early.
- **2006** — An under-12 team completes the pathway.
- **2010s** — The first team's ideas about stopping and passing the ball spread to the academy. TANAKA Ao, ITAKURA Kou and MITOMA Kaoru come through in these years.
- **2013** — A first J Youth Cup semi-final.
- **2016–2017** — Third place in the Club Youth Championship two years running.
- **2021** — Prince League Kanto champions in the club's twelfth season in that league, then promotion through the Premier League play-off.
- **2022** — Premier League EAST champions at the first attempt; runners-up in the national final.
- **2023** — Anker Frontown Ikuta opens. The under-15 level splits into two teams, Ikuta and Todoroki, to widen recruitment across the city.
- **2024** — Runners-up in the Club Youth Championship. The under-15 team wins its own national club youth title the same summer.

## model

- **One third of the first team.** The academy's published targets include a specific one: that players from the academy should make up at least a third of the first-team squad. In September 2026 the first-team list on the J.League site included twenty-one current academy players registered as type-2 players — FUJITA Asuto and OGAWA Hiroto with first-team numbers — alongside the graduates already there.
- **More than one road up.** The club states plainly that not everyone goes straight from the under-18s to the first team. Some sign professional contracts while still at the academy; others go to university and come back. MITOMA went via the University of Tsukuba, WAKIZAKA Yasuto via Hannan University, YAMADA Shin and HAYASAKA Yuki via Toin University of Yokohama.
- **Everything on one site.** Since 2023 the under-12, under-15 and under-18 teams all train at Anker Frontown Ikuta, which has a full-size artificial pitch, an indoor arena and facilities for meals after training. Younger players see the under-18 standard every day.
- **School first.** The academy puts schoolwork at the start of its definition of player development, on the reasoning that the thinking a player does in class is the same thinking he does in a match.

- **MITOMA Kaoru** (winger, Brighton & Hove Albion) — turned down promotion from the under-18s to go to university, joined the first team four years later, and moved to Europe via Union Saint-Gilloise.
- **TAKAI Kota** (defender, Tottenham Hotspur) — from the under-12s to the first team, and to the Premier League in 2025.
- **MIYOSHI Koji** (midfielder) — Antwerp, Birmingham City and Bochum in Europe.
- **MIYASHIRO Taisei** (forward, Vissel Kobe) and **YAMADA Shin** (forward, Celtic) — both called up by Japan for the 2025 EAFF E-1 Championship.
- **WAKIZAKA Yasuto** (midfielder, Kawasaki Frontale) — the club's number 14.
- **OZEKI Yuto**, **MIYAGI Ten**, **YUI Kota**, **MATSUNAGANE Yuto** and **HAYASAKA Yuki** — academy graduates in the current first-team squad.
