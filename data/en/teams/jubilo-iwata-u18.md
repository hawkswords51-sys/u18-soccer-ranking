## intro

Jubilo Iwata U-18 is the under-18 team of Jubilo Iwata, the club from Iwata in Shizuoka, one of Japan's traditional football heartlands. In 1999 it won both of the national youth titles of the time, the Japan Club Youth Championship and the All Japan Youth (U-18) Championship. Two of its graduates, ITO Hiroki and GOTO Keisuke, were in Japan's squad for the 2026 World Cup. After six seasons in the Premier League from 2018 to 2023, the team is back in the Premier League WEST in 2026.

## style

The club's under-15 teams are spread across Shizuoka Prefecture — Iwata itself plus CENTRAL, EAST and WEST teams — and most of the under-18 squad comes up through them. In 2026 the team is led by AMMA Takayoshi, who coached the under-18s in 2025, took charge of the first team later that year, and returned to the academy for 2026. Since July 2026 he has also coached the club's new under-21 team, and in August the club registered more than thirty under-18 players with the first team so that they can play in the new U-21 J.League as well.

## history

- **1998–1999** — Third in the Club Youth Championship in 1998, then champions in 1999, the same year the team won the All Japan Youth Championship.
- **Early 2000s** — A strong run of graduates into the first team, and a second pair of national finals in 2004.
- **2017 school year** — ITO Hiroki graduates into the first team.
- **2018–2023** — Six seasons in the Premier League WEST.
- **2022 school year** — GOTO Keisuke graduates into the first team.
- **2025** — AMMA Takayoshi becomes under-18 head coach, then first-team head coach later in the year.
- **2026** — AMMA returns to the under-18s and the team returns to the Premier League WEST. In March, NISHIOKA Kento signs a professional contract while still an under-18 player; in August, OKUDA Yuma is confirmed to join the first team in January 2027.

## model

- **A network of under-15 teams across Shizuoka.** Besides the main under-15 team in Iwata, the club runs under-15 teams in the centre, east and west of the prefecture, so players from all over Shizuoka can follow the same path into the under-18s.
- **Professional contracts before graduation.** NISHIOKA Kento signed a professional contract in March 2026 and took first-team number 17, while continuing to play for the under-18s.
- **A route through the U-21 team.** From 2026 the club has an under-21 team in the new U-21 J.League, coached by the under-18 head coach, and most of the under-18 squad is registered to play in it.
- **A link with Germany.** The club has a partnership with VfL Bochum.

## alumni

- **ITO Hiroki** (defender, Bayern Munich) — through the under-15 and under-18 teams to the first team (class of the 2017 school year), and in Japan's 2026 World Cup squad.
- **GOTO Keisuke** (forward, Sint-Truiden) — through the under-15 and under-18 teams to the first team (class of the 2022 school year), and Japan's number 9 at the 2026 World Cup.
- **NAKAJIMA Keitaro** (goalkeeper), **UEHARA Rikiya** (midfielder), **KAWAI Tokumo** (midfielder), **KAI Yua** (defender) and **ISHIZUKA Rea** (midfielder) — academy graduates in Jubilo Iwata's first-team squad in 2026.
