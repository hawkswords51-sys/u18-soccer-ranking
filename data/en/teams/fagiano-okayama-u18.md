## intro

Fagiano Okayama U-18 is the under-18 team of Fagiano Okayama, from Okayama in western Japan. It is one of the newer academies in the Premier League: in 2023 it won the Prince League Chugoku for the first time, won the play-off for promotion, and finished third at the Japan Club Youth Championship. It has played in the Premier League WEST since 2024. In 2025 two of its second-year high-school players, SUEMUNE Kanshiro and SENDA Ryo, were promoted to the first team — a first for the club.

## style

The team trains at the Masada football ground in eastern Okayama, which the first team also uses. From 2026 it is coached by ABE Yuta, who led Vissel Kobe U-18 to the Premier League WEST title in 2025. The club has two under-15 teams, Masada and Yorishima, and most of the squad comes up through them; the rest is recruited mainly from around the Chugoku and Shikoku regions.

## history

- **2013 school year** — The first under-18 graduates are promoted to the first team.
- **2017** — The team enters the Prince League Chugoku, finishing third in its second season.
- **2023** — Prince League Chugoku champions, promotion through the play-off, and third place at the Club Youth Championship.
- **2024** — A first season in the Premier League WEST, finishing tenth.
- **2025** — In February, SUEMUNE Kanshiro and SENDA Ryo are promoted to the first team as second-year high-school students and sign professional contracts. The team again finishes tenth.
- **2026** — ABE Yuta becomes head coach. From February to May, SUEMUNE and SENDA spend three months on a short-term placement at Maringá FC in Brazil.

## model

- **An early route to the first team.** SUEMUNE and SENDA signed professional contracts while still second-years at high school and stayed registered as academy players. Both were students at Okayama Gakugeikan High School.
- **Links abroad.** The Brazil placement was part of what the club calls strengthening its connections overseas and developing young players.
- **Shared training ground.** The under-18s train at Masada, alongside the first team.
- **A regional recruiting base.** Besides the club's own under-15 players, the 2026 squad includes players from Kagawa, Hiroshima, Yamaguchi and Shimane, and from the under-15 teams of Cerezo Osaka and FC Ryukyu.

## alumni

- **SUEMUNE Kanshiro** (forward, Fagiano Okayama) — through the club's under-15 and under-18 teams, promoted in 2025 as a second-year. Has played for Japan at under-17 and under-18 level.
- **SENDA Ryo** (defender, Fagiano Okayama) — the same path and the same promotion in 2025; also a Japan under-17 and under-18 international.
- **OTA Ryunosuke** (forward, on loan at Jubilo Iwata) — from the Okayama under-18s to Meiji University and then Fagiano Okayama's first team; playing on loan at Jubilo Iwata in 2026/27.
