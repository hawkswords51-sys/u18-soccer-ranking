Japan U-18 Prince Leagues 2026 Standings

The Prince Leagues are the second tier of under-18 football in Japan: 13 leagues across 9 regions, played from April to December by high-school clubs and J.League academies alike. Most of the schools that reach the winter All Japan High School Soccer Tournament play here, so these tables are the best guide to how strong those teams are. Standings and the latest results are updated daily from official JFA data.

In December the leading Prince League teams enter a play-off for four places in the Premier League, and the bottom teams are relegated to their prefectural leagues. The number of places changes from year to year, so no promotion or relegation zones are marked here.

How to read these tables

Pos = position, Pts = points (3 for a win, 1 for a draw), P = played, W/D/L = won/drawn/lost, GF/GA = goals for/against, GD = goal difference. Pref. is the prefecture where the team is based. Team names link to our English team profiles; the Japanese profiles linked from each of them add history, notable alumni and current squads.

One level above: Premier League standings (EAST and WEST).
