Sagan Tosu U-18

Sagan Tosu U-18 is the under-18 team of Sagan Tosu, a J.League club from a town of about 75,000 people in Saga, the smallest prefecture in Kyushu. It has no big city and no big company behind it, and for years it lost the best local teenagers to famous school teams in Nagasaki and Fukuoka. In 2022 it became the national champion of the Premier League, beating Kawasaki Frontale U-18 3-2 at the National Stadium. Since then it has become known for something else as well: sending players straight from its academy to Europe.

Saga · head coach HIDAKA Takuma

2026 season

Currently 4th in the Premier League WEST with 24 points from 14 matches (7W 3D 4L, 21-14). Updated daily.

Playing style

Two things are combined. The first is the club's founding character — running, fighting, never giving up — which every Tosu team is expected to show. The second came from a partnership with Ajax that began in 2018: positional play, decision-making and creativity, and the idea that players should be able to solve problems themselves. The result is a team that can press high and hard, but can also sit in a compact mid-block and let the opponent have the ball when that is the better option, as it did in the 2022 final. HIDAKA Takuma is the under-18 head coach.

The 2022 final was the first played at the National Stadium in Tokyo. Tosu went a goal down, equalised just before half-time, and scored twice early in the second half; the captain, FUKUI Taichi, scored the third.

History

  • 1997 — Sagan Tosu is founded after the collapse of the town's previous professional club, and an academy begins with it.
  • 2000s–2010s — A long period as the underdog. The best players in the region choose famous school teams; Tosu builds its identity on hard work.
  • 2018 — The partnership with Ajax begins, including an exchange trip to the Netherlands for the junior-youth team.
  • 2019 — Prince League Kyushu champions and runners-up in the Club Youth Championship; promotion to the Premier League for 2020.
  • 2020 — The Club Youth Championship, the first national title at this age.
  • 2022 — Premier League WEST champions, then national champions.
  • 2023 — FUKUI Taichi moves to Bayern Munich.
  • 2026 — SHINKAWA Shion, promoted from the academy in 2025, moves to Sint-Truiden in Belgium. IZAWA Riku signs a professional contract as a second-year.

Development pathway

  • Two junior-youth teams. Besides the main under-15 team in Tosu, the club runs a second one in Karatsu, on the other side of the prefecture, so that talent in Saga and neighbouring Fukuoka and Nagasaki is not missed. Players from both reach the under-18s.
  • Recruiting beyond Kyushu. The 2026 squad includes players from academies in Osaka, Shiga, Nagano and Aichi, and one from FC Seoul's youth system in South Korea.
  • A dormitory and a partner school. Players from outside the area live in the club's dormitory, where meals and daily routines are managed, and study at a partner high school in Saga that has its own strong football team.
  • Straight to Europe. FUKUI went to Bayern Munich at 18; SHINKAWA to Sint-Truiden at 19. The club treats this as part of its model: the players get the career they want, and the solidarity and training payments help to fund the academy.

Former players

  • FUKUI Taichi (midfielder, Arouca) — through the club's under-12, under-15 and under-18 teams, captain and scorer in the 2022 final, then Bayern Munich and, from 2025, Arouca in Portugal.
  • SHINKAWA Shion (forward, Sint-Truiden) — promoted in 2025, called up by Japan's under-22s, and moved to Belgium in January 2026.
  • NARAHARA Yoshiki (midfielder, Sagan Tosu) — from the club's under-12s, part of the 2022 champion team, promoted in 2023.
  • SAKAIYA Keisuke (midfielder, Sagan Tosu) — promoted in 2024.
  • SUZUKI Daichi (forward, Sagan Tosu) — promoted in 2025, now wearing the number 10.
  • MIZUMAKI Tokito (midfielder, Sagan Tosu) — promoted in 2026.

The academy has sent at least one player to the first team almost every year since 2019.
