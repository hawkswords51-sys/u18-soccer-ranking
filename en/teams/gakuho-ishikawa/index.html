Gakuho Ishikawa High School

Gakuho Ishikawa High School (学法石川高校) is a high school football team from Fukushima Prefecture, Japan. In 2026 it plays in the Prince League Tohoku, one of the regional Prince Leagues that form the second tier of under-18 league football in Japan, directly below the national Premier League.
