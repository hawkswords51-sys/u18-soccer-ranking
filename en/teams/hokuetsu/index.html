Hokuetsu High School

Hokuetsu High School (北越高校) is a high school football team from Niigata Prefecture, Japan. In 2026 it plays in the Prince League Hokushinetsu Division 2, one of the regional Prince Leagues that form the second tier of under-18 league football in Japan, directly below the national Premier League.
