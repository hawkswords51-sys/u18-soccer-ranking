Tamano Konan High School

Tamano Konan High School (玉野光南高校) is a high school football team from Okayama Prefecture, Japan. In 2026 it plays in the Prince League Chugoku, one of the regional Prince Leagues that form the second tier of under-18 league football in Japan, directly below the national Premier League.
