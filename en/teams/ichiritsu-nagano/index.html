Ichiritsu Nagano High School

Ichiritsu Nagano High School (市立長野高校; full name 長野市立長野高等学校, and also called Nagano City High School on the school's website) is a public school run by the city of Nagano, the capital of Nagano Prefecture in central Japan, and the only city-run high school in the prefecture. It took its present form in 2008, when the city's Satsuki High School was relaunched as Ichiritsu Nagano, and the football club was founded in the same school year. On 8 November 2026 the team drew 0-0 with Matsumoto Kokusai after extra time in the final of the Nagano qualifying tournament and won the penalty shoot-out 4-2. It will play at the winter All Japan High School Soccer Tournament for the second time, five years after its first appearance. In 2021–22 it became the first team from northern Nagano (the Hokushin area) to win the prefectural title. It has played twice at the summer Inter-High and reached the round of 16 in 2017.

Playing style

The team is known for keeping the ball. A local sports website describes its football as possession-based, with players changing their positions during the game to pass their way through the opponent, and Gekisaka wrote in 2021 of the club's "trademark high-tempo possession football". The current head coach, who joined the staff as an assistant and took charge in April 2025, has said that keeping the ball remains the team's character, but that running, quick transitions and intensity come first, and that the team will also play long balls when needed. His predecessor led the club from 2012 to the 2024 school year before moving to Shohei High School in Saitama.

The 2026 team won its winter qualifying matches narrowly. Entering in the fourth round as a seed, it beat Ueda Higashi, Ueda and Tokyo City University Shiojiri, each 2-1. In the semi-final against Tokyo City University Shiojiri at Sunpro Alwin, a third-year midfielder headed the winner in stoppage time. The final, also at Sunpro Alwin, stayed goalless through extra time. Matsumoto Kokusai had beaten Ichiritsu Nagano 3-0 in the Inter-High qualifying final in June.

History

  • 2008 — Satsuki High School is relaunched as Ichiritsu Nagano High School, with an integrated course, and the football club is founded.
  • 2012 — A new head coach takes over the club in its fifth year. He leads it for 13 seasons.
  • 2014 — The school begins a partnership with AC Nagano Parceiro, the city's J.League club.
  • 2016 — In its ninth year the club wins the Nagano Inter-High qualifiers, beating Tokyo City University Shiojiri 4-1 in the final. At the national finals it loses 1-5 to Setouchi in the first round.
  • 2017 — A second Inter-High title in Nagano. At the national finals in Miyagi the team records its first win, 2-1 against Kashima Gakuen, then draws 2-2 with Rissho Shonan, equalising with a header seven minutes into stoppage time, and wins on penalties. It loses 0-5 to Ryutsu Keizai University Kashiwa in the third round, but it is the first Nagano team in the last 16 for 22 years. In the winter it loses the Nagano final 1-2 to Ueda Nishi.
  • 2021 — At the 100th winter championship the team beats Matsumoto Kokusai 2-0 in the Nagano semi-finals and comes from 1-2 down at half-time to beat Nagano Nichidai 3-2 in the final, with a hat-trick from a third-year forward. It is the first winter title for a team from northern Nagano, and the first for a public school in the prefecture for 24 years. At the national championship it plays its first match in the second round and loses 0-2 to Higashiyama.
  • 2023–2025 — Runners-up in the 2023 Inter-High qualifiers. In the 2024 winter qualifiers the team beats Matsumoto Kokusai 3-1 in the semi-finals and loses the final to Ueda Nishi on penalties (2-2, 6-7). A new head coach takes over in April 2025, and the team reaches the winter semi-finals.
  • 2026 — Runners-up in the Nagano Inter-High qualifiers (0-3 to Matsumoto Kokusai), then second in Division 1 with 10 wins, one draw and three defeats. In November it wins the Nagano winter qualifiers.

Development pathway

For readers new to Japanese youth football: high-school teams and J.League academies play in the same national league pyramid. At the top are the U-18 Premier League (EAST and WEST), then nine regional Prince Leagues, then the prefectural leagues. Ichiritsu Nagano plays in Division 1 of Nagano's prefectural U-18 league, one level below the Prince League Hokushin'etsu. After finishing second in 2026 it will play in the Hokushin'etsu play-offs on 22 and 23 November, where the two block winners earn places in Division 2 of the 2027 Prince League. Two high-school tournaments carry even more prestige than the league: the summer Inter-High and the winter championship, which is televised nationally and ends at the National Stadium in January.

  • A link with the local J.League club. Since 2014 the school has worked with AC Nagano Parceiro. Several players have come to Ichiritsu Nagano from Parceiro's under-15 team, and two of its professionals spent their first high-school year in Parceiro's under-18 team before moving to the school's football club.
  • Local players. The squad is drawn from clubs and junior high schools around the prefecture, such as Nagano FC Garf, FC Nakano Esperanza and Antelope Shiojiri.
  • A northern outlier. In 2026 it was the only Division 1 team from the Hokushin area around Nagano city; the other seven came from central, eastern and southern Nagano.

Former players

  • ARAI Hikaru (midfielder, FC Imabari) — played for the school in 2016 and 2017 after a year in AC Nagano Parceiro's under-18 team, and scored the stoppage-time equaliser against Rissho Shonan at the 2017 Inter-High. He joined Shonan Bellmare as a designated special player while still at school and has also played for Gainare Tottori and Fukushima United. He has represented Japan at under-15 and under-16 level.
  • YAMANAKA Reo (midfielder, AC Nagano Parceiro) — scored twice in the 2016 Nagano Inter-High final. He went on to Takushoku University and joined Parceiro, where he wears the number 10 in 2026.

A third former player, the captain of the 2021–22 team, joined AC Nagano Parceiro from Kanagawa University. His name is not romanised here because no English spelling has been published by the JFA, the J.League or the club. No Ichiritsu Nagano graduate was in Japan's squad for the 2026 World Cup.
