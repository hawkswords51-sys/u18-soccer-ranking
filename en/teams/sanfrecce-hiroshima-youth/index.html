Sanfrecce Hiroshima F.C Youth

Sanfrecce Hiroshima F.C Youth is the under-18 team of Sanfrecce Hiroshima, and the most successful club academy in the history of the Premier League: national champions in 2011, 2012 and 2018 (a record shared only with Aomori Yamada High School), and WEST champions six times. It is also unusual in how it works. The players do not live at home and commute to a training ground in the city; they live together in the club's dormitory in Yoshida, a small town in Akitakata in the hills north of Hiroshima, and go to the local prefectural high school. The first-team goalkeeper, OSAKO Keisuke, came through this system and is a Japan international.

Hiroshima · head coach SAWADA Kentaro

2026 season

Currently 2nd in the Premier League WEST with 27 points from 14 matches (8W 3D 3L, 24-18). Updated daily.

Playing style

The club's teams are built on precise technique first — control and passing under pressure — with, in recent seasons, a high-pressing, fast-transition game layered on top. The first team and the academy use the same tactical language, so that a player who is promoted does not have to learn a new game. SAWADA Kentaro, a former Sanfrecce player who is also the club's academy director, took charge of the under-18s in February 2026. His emphasis is on versatility: players who can play more than one position well.

History

  • 1992 — Sanfrecce Hiroshima is founded as one of the original J.League clubs. Its first general manager argues that a club in a regional city cannot keep buying players, and puts the academy at the centre of the plan.
  • 1995 — A first J Youth Cup.
  • 2003–2004 — The Club Youth Championship twice in a row, and in 2004 the All Japan Youth Championship, beating Jubilo Iwata Youth 1-0 in the final at Saitama Stadium.
  • 2010 — A second All Japan Youth title, 2-1 against FC Tokyo U-18.
  • 2011–2012 — The Premier League is created, and Sanfrecce win the WEST and the national final in each of its first two seasons.
  • 2016 — WEST champions again; the final is lost to Aomori Yamada on penalties.
  • 2018 — A third national title, 2-1 against Kashima Antlers Youth.
  • 2021, 2023 — Two more WEST titles. The 2021 final is cancelled; the 2023 final is lost 2-1 to Aomori Yamada.
  • 2026 — SAWADA Kentaro becomes head coach. Five of the under-18 squad hold first-team numbers.

Development pathway

  • Everyone lives in. Around forty players live in the Mitsuya dormitory in Yoshida and attend Hiroshima Prefectural Yoshida High School. The club's view is that living away from home, doing ordinary schoolwork at an ordinary school, and training at a professional level together produce more independent people, and better players, than football alone.
  • Recruited from everywhere. Because the players board, the club can take them from anywhere in Japan. The 2026 squad includes four players from the same junior-youth club in Kumamoto, and others from Tokyo, Chiba, Kyoto, Osaka, Tokushima and Kochi, alongside those from the club's own junior-youth team.
  • A short step to the first team. In September 2026 five current youth players had first-team squad numbers: NOGUCHI Rento, HARA Minato, OTA Hiroto, MORII Leedo and TAKAHASHI Narumi. NOGUCHI has already been promoted for the 2026-27 season.
  • Former players on the staff. Several of the club's best-known academy graduates now work in the academy, as coaches or scouts, so the club's way of playing is taught by people who learned it there.
