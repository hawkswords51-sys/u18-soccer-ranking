Hokkaido Consadole Sapporo U-18

Hokkaido Consadole Sapporo U-18 is the under-18 team of Hokkaido Consadole Sapporo, the professional club of Japan's northernmost island, and the strongest club academy in a region that covers more than a fifth of the country's land area. It won the EAST conference of the national Premier League in the competition's first season, 2011, and the J.League Youth Cup in 2012. Since dropping back to the regional tier in 2016 it has been the most consistent side in Prince League Hokkaido, and on 26 September 2026 a 1-0 win over Asahikawa Jitsugyo High School, the second-placed team, secured the 2026 title with a round to spare. The team now goes into the December play-off for promotion to the Premier League. Like all J.League academies, Consadole's under-18s do not take part in the high school competitions — the Inter-High in summer and the winter All Japan High School Championship. Their national competitions are the league pyramid, the Japan Club Youth (U-18) Championship and the J.League Youth Cup.

Playing style

Football in Hokkaido is shaped by the climate. Snow covers the pitches for several months of the year, so the season has to be planned around long winters, indoor work and trips south, and the island's size means that away matches inside Hokkaido can involve long journeys. The academy's answer has been to centralise: talented players identified across the island at the club's junior-youth bases are brought together in Sapporo for their high school years, and the under-18s train at the club's facilities at Higashi-Karinai in the east of the city.

The 2026 side has been built to be hard to beat. After 13 league matches it was unbeaten, with nine wins and four draws (31 points), and the title was decided by a 1-0 win in the head-to-head with Asahikawa Jitsugyo, which left Consadole four points clear with one round to play. The season was not an easy one off the pitch: in April 2026 the club dismissed the under-18 head coach after an investigation found harassment of staff and players, and the team finished the league campaign under his replacement.

Some context for readers outside Japan. The national Premier League (EAST and WEST, twelve teams each) sits above nine regional Prince Leagues; Prince League Hokkaido is the regional tier for the island, and its champion goes to an end-of-season play-off with the other regional champions for a place in the Premier League. High school teams and club academies play in the same leagues. The Club Youth Championship is the national cup for club teams, traditionally held in summer, and the J.League Youth Cup is a separate competition for the academies of J.League clubs. In 2012, the year of the Youth Cup win, the club also received the J.League award for the best youth development club.

History

  • 1996 — Consadole Sapporo is founded as the professional club for Hokkaido.
  • 1997 — The academy begins, with an under-18 and an under-15 team, based at the Fuji Sports Plaza in Kuriyama, a town east of Sapporo. An under-12 team follows in 2002, and the academy later moves to Higashi-Karinai in Sapporo.
  • 2001 — Runners-up in the Club Youth Championship, beaten in the final by FC Tokyo U-18.
  • 2011 — The Premier League is created as a national tier. Consadole win the EAST conference in its first season, finishing three points clear of Tokyo Verdy Youth and Shimizu S-Pulse Youth, and lose the national final 3-1 to Sanfrecce Hiroshima Youth at Saitama Stadium 2002.
  • 2012 — The J.League Youth Cup, won 5-1 against Gamba Osaka Youth in the final at Nagai Stadium in Osaka on 24 December — the club's first national title at this age group.
  • 2015 — Ninth in the Premier League EAST and relegated after five seasons.
  • 2016–2017 — Back-to-back Prince League Hokkaido titles.
  • 2019 — Another Prince League Hokkaido title. SHINDO Ryosuke and SUGA Daiki, two academy graduates, are called up to the Japan national team in the same year.
  • 2021 — Prince League Hokkaido champions, and runners-up in the Club Youth Championship, losing the final to Nagoya Grampus U-18.
  • 2024 — A fifth Prince League Hokkaido title.
  • 2025 — Sapporo Otani High School win Prince League Hokkaido.
  • 2026 — The title is regained, clinched with one round to play by a 1-0 win over Asahikawa Jitsugyo on 26 September.

Development pathway

  • One academy for the whole island. Hokkaido is too large for one training centre to cover. Besides Sapporo, the club runs junior-youth (under-15) teams in Asahikawa, Kushiro and Muroran, and under-12 teams in Higashikawa, Kushiro and Muroran, so that boys far from the city can play in the club's system. The best of them then move up to the single under-18 team in Sapporo.
  • Open to outsiders at sixteen. The under-18s are not only a continuation of the club's own junior-youth teams. SHINDO Ryosuke and NAKAMURA Toya, for example, both joined at high school age from other junior-youth clubs in Sapporo and went on to play for the first team.
  • A route through university. Not every player is offered a professional contract at eighteen, and the club keeps track of those who go to university. KIDO Shuma and SATO Yosei both went from the under-18s to Osaka University of Health and Sport Sciences before joining the first team, KAWAHARA Hayato came back via Kokushikan University, and SAKURABA Riki went via the University of Tsukuba to a professional career elsewhere.
  • A first team built on its own graduates. In autumn 2026 the Consadole first-team squad, now playing in J2, includes eight academy graduates who played for the under-18 team: ARANO Takuma, HORIGOME Yuto, NISHINO Shota, NAKAMURA Toya, KIDO Shuma, SATO Yosei, KAWAHARA Hayato and goalkeeper TADANO Kakuma, who was promoted straight from the under-18s for 2026.

Former players

  • NISHI Daigo (defender) — came through the club's youth teams and turned professional with Consadole in 2006; went on to Kashima Antlers, Vissel Kobe and Urawa Red Diamonds, returned to Sapporo, played for Japan, and retired at the end of the 2025 season with Iwate Grulla Morioka.
  • ARANO Takuma (midfielder, Hokkaido Consadole Sapporo) — a product of the club's under-15 and under-18 teams who made his J.League debut at 17 in 2010 and has spent his whole career at the club.
  • HORIGOME Yuto (midfielder, Hokkaido Consadole Sapporo) — under-12 to under-18 at the club, then Consadole, Fukushima United and Albirex Niigata, where he was named in the 2022 J2 Best XI; back in Sapporo in 2026.
  • SUGA Daiki (midfielder, Sanfrecce Hiroshima) — went from the under-12s all the way to the first team, and won a cap for Japan.
  • SHINDO Ryosuke (defender, V-Varen Nagasaki) — joined the under-18s from SSS Sapporo's junior-youth team; Consadole and Cerezo Osaka, and called up to the national team in 2019.
  • NISHINO Shota (defender, Hokkaido Consadole Sapporo) — another under-12-to-first-team graduate, who made his J.League debut at 17 in 2021; after a spell at Kamatamare Sanuki he is now a first-team regular.
  • NAKAMURA Toya (defender, Hokkaido Consadole Sapporo) — joined the under-18s from ASC Hokkaido, and gained experience with Honda FC before establishing himself in Sapporo.
  • SAKURABA Riki (goalkeeper, Tochigi SC) — under-15 and under-18 at the club, then the University of Tsukuba, FC Osaka and Gainare Tottori.
  • KIDO Shuma and SATO Yosei (Hokkaido Consadole Sapporo) — both left the under-18s for Osaka University of Health and Sport Sciences and came back as professionals.
  • FUJIMURA Ren and IZUMA Shido (Thespa Gunma) — FUJIMURA joined Consadole from the under-18s and has also played for Montedio Yamagata and Iwate Grulla Morioka; IZUMA, under-12 to under-18 at the club, is at Gunma on loan from Consadole.
  • TADANO Kakuma (goalkeeper, Hokkaido Consadole Sapporo) — the 193 cm goalkeeper promoted from the under-18s for 2026, the most recent graduate.
