Konan High School

Konan High School (興南高校) is a private school in Naha, the capital of Okinawa Prefecture, founded in 1962. It is best known nationally for its baseball team; its football club calls itself Konan FC. On 7 November 2026 the football team beat Ginowan 2-1 after extra time in the final of the Okinawa qualifying tournament. It is the first time a private school has won the Okinawa title, and Konan will play at the winter All Japan High School Soccer Tournament for the first time in 2026–27. Okinawa sends one team to the winter championship, so the prefectural title is the only way in. Konan has not yet played at the summer Inter-High. It plays in Division 1 of Okinawa's prefectural U-18 league (the "Habu League"), which it joined in 2024.

Playing style

The club's vision is "to develop people who will raise up the south through football" and "to aim to be the best high-school football club in Japan". Its motto, "bunbu rendo", means that study and sport should shape each other. The head coach, a former football-magazine reporter, rebuilt the team in 2014, when it had only 16 players. He uses video analysis and has said that the aim is to raise the players' football intelligence as well as their technique, fitness and character.

The 2026 team won the Okinawa title by defending well. It kept clean sheets in its first four matches, from the second round to the semi-finals, which it won 4-3 on penalties against Nago after a 0-0 draw. In the final at the Okinawa Prefectural Comprehensive Athletic Park stadium in Okinawa City, a forward put Konan ahead in the 36th minute from a scramble in front of goal. Ginowan equalised in the second half, but five minutes into extra time a substitute midfielder scored the winner with a shot from outside the penalty area. The players threw a coach into the air in celebration, and the captain said it was "a title we won by defending and attacking together".

History

  • 2014 — The current head coach takes over and rebuilds a team of 16 players.
  • 2021–2023 — Three consecutive quarter-final appearances in the Okinawa winter qualifiers. In 2023 the team wins Division 2 of the prefectural league and is promoted.
  • 2024 — In its first season in Division 1 the team finishes second, according to the club. It reaches the Okinawa Inter-High final, where it leads Naha Nishi 2-0 but loses 2-4 after extra time, and plays at the Kyushu championship, losing to Ryukoku of Saga on penalties. It also reaches the semi-finals of the winter qualifiers, losing 1-3 to Ginowan.
  • 2025 — Semi-finals of the Inter-High qualifiers and a third-round exit in the winter qualifiers.
  • 2026 — Konan beats the two-time holders Naha Nishi 2-0 in the Inter-High quarter-finals but loses 0-1 to KBC High School in the semi-finals. In the autumn it wins the Okinawa winter qualifiers for the first time, beating Ginowan in the final.

Development pathway

For readers new to Japanese youth football: high-school teams and J.League academies play in the same national league pyramid. At the top are the U-18 Premier League (EAST and WEST), then nine regional Prince Leagues, then the prefectural leagues. Konan plays in Division 1 of Okinawa's prefectural U-18 league, one level below the Prince League Kyushu. Two high-school tournaments carry even more prestige than the league: the summer Inter-High and the winter championship, which is televised nationally and ends at the National Stadium in January.

  • Okinawa's public-school tradition. The prefecture's winter title had always gone to public schools until Konan won it in 2026. In the summer of 2026 another private school, KBC High School, won the Okinawa Inter-High qualifiers.
  • Young players. Konan has won the Okinawa U-16 League twice, in 2022 and 2024, and four of its first-year players were picked for the Okinawa under-16 regional squad in 2026.
  • Not to be confused with Kokoku. Kokoku High School in Osaka, whose former players include several J.League and Japan players, is a different school.
